My Childhood

Growing up in Hong Kong has shaped who I am today. Located in the Southern part of China, Hong Kong is a special administrative city, which is under a governing system called "One Country, Two Systems". Due to this policy, Hong Kong is governed through different systems compared to in mainland China. Having grown up in this environment, I got to experience a unique blend of Eastern traditions and Western influences in everyday life. English and Chinese were both commonly used in school and in public spaces, which helped me develop strong communication skills in a bilingual setting. The fast-paced lifestyle of the city, combined with its strong emphasis on education and hard work, encouraged me to become disciplined, independent, and adaptable. At the same time, Hong Kong’s rich cultural heritage, festivals, and close-knit community values taught me the importance of tradition, family, and cultural identity.

National pride, to me, is not only about geography or politics, but mainly about culture, traditions, language, and shared experiences. I still remember celebrating dragon boat festival with my grandmother, and making Zong Zi (rice dumplings) and watching the dragon boat races on television. These traditions allow me to connect to my Chinese roots, yet, at the same time, growing up in such a global environment has taught me to remain open-minded and accepting of different viewpoints, cultures, and traditions of others. Rather than being fixated on learning more about my own culture, I also should engage and learn more about other cultures to expand my own knowledge and learn to build an identity for myself.

My Hobbies

Artistic Hobbies

As a student in Tisch and a Game Design major, I have various interests in the arts. Some of them include:

  • Photography
  • Videography / Filming
  • Vlogging
  • Pixel Art + Pixel Art Animation
  • Drawing / Sketching / Digital Art
  • Game Development / Design

Something that I am currently very passionate about is photography and game development. Over the past few years, photography has become something I had fallen in love with. A genre of photography that I quite enjoy is street photography. Having grown up in a big city, and now currently living in another iconic city, London, I like taking photos of the small, seemingly, insignificant aspects of the city, and turning them into moments of beauty.

Another hobby that I am currently very interested in is game development. Over the past few years, designing games has become something I have truly grown to love. What draws me most to game design is the ability to create interactive experiences that bring stories, ideas, and emotions to life. I enjoy thinking about how mechanics, visuals, and sound can work together to immerse players in a world that feels engaging and meaningful. Whether it is designing small gameplay systems or developing larger concepts, I am fascinated by how thoughtful design can turn simple ideas into memorable experiences.
